# DGOC Tower Crane Planner pluginSourced tower-crane catalogue, capacity model, placement and clearance checks for the DGOC CranePlanner. The catalogue contains four entries:- **Potain MCT 88** (EN 14439 C25) — Cycle 2 idealised tip-load model. Default entry.- **Potain MCT 85 F5** (FEM 1.001-A3) — published per-radius load-chart lookup.- **JASO J438PA.2R, 213 ft TD** (EN 14439 C25 / FEM 1005-C25) — `INDICATIVE` luffing-jib  configuration. Listed but **not evaluated** by the trolley-jib capacity engine.- **JASO J438PA.2R, 213 ft 2R** — the second `INDICATIVE` configuration of the same JASO model,  also listed but not evaluated.The two JASO rows are configurations of one crane model, not two separate models. The 213 ft TD/2Rchoice is `INDICATIVE` because the datasheet also publishes shorter jibs; the chart values themselvesare transcribed from the PDF.> **Planning aid, not a certified lift plan.**## What is here| Path | Purpose || --- | --- || `data/crane-catalogue.json` | Crane data. For theMCT 88 with a 30 m jib it gives `M = 81 t·m`, `r_c = 16.2 m`, and `capacity(30 m) = 2.70 t`.### MCT 85 F5 — published chart lookupThe MCT 85 F5 uses its own manufacturer chart, not the MCT 88 formula:- The installed jib must be exactly one of the eight published configurations; otherwise the result  is `INVALID`.- The printed minimum hook radius is 2.7 m and is stored on every chart row. A radius below 2.7 m is  `OUT_OF_RANGE`; the 5 t plateau does not extend below the published minimum.- The base capacity is read from that jib's chart row. From 2.7 m to the first stored point the 5 t  plateau applies; the first stored point is the end of the plateau, not the minimum radius.- Between chart points, the capacity at the **next larger radius** is used (the lower bounding value).  This cannot overstate the published capacity, though it may understate it between points.- There is no extrapolation beyond the last published radius.- The chart is the 5 t hook curve; the dashed 2.5 t-hook variant is not modelled.For example, on the 30 m jib, 19 m returns 3.9 t (the 20 m point), not the linearly interpolated4.48 t; 24 m returns 3.0 t (the 25 m point); 28 m returns 2.5 t (the 29 m point).### JASO J438PA.2R — listed but not evaluatedThe JASO entries are **luffing-jib** cranes. Their published charts are stored with p. 2 provenance,but the current capacity engine models a trolley jib and cannot represent luffing geometry. For theseentries:- `capacityAt` returns `null`, even for a radius printed in the chart;- a pick is reported as `UNSUPPORTED` with issue `unsupported-crane-type`, not as PASS/FAIL;- the selection matrix lists the rows but excludes them from `evaluatedCraneCount` and never counts  them as passing; when every entry is luffing-jib the matrix state is `no-supported-cranes`.The safeguard is deliberate. It prevents an imperial luffing-jib chart from being read through thetrolley-jib formula and presented as an equivalent capacity result. See[`docs/SOURCES.md`](docs/SOURCES.md) §6.### WindThe optional wind rule multiplies the base capacity by 0.8 at 20 km/h or above. It is labelled`SIMULATED` in the code and UI and is not claimed to be EN 14439 Table 7 or a manufacturer rule. It isapplied once, after the crane-specific base capacity. A blank or `null` wind means no derate; anegative or non-finite wind is `INVALID` with issue `invalid-wind` instead of being treated as calm.## Sample picksThe MCT 88 and MCT 85 F5 tables use the catalogue default 30 m jib.**MCT 88**| Pick | Load | Radius | Capacity | Utilisation | Result || --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- || 1 | 2.5 t | 20 m | 4.05 t | 61.7 % | PASS || 2 | 3.0 t | 25 m | 3.24 t | 92.6 % | PASS || 3 | 4.0 t | 30 m | 2.70 t | 148.1 % | FAIL |The third row is the acceptance case from `BACKLOG.md`: a 4 t pick at 30 m on the 30 m jib must fail.**MCT 85 F5**| Pick | Load | Radius | Capacity | Utilisation | Result || --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- || 1 | 2.5 t | 20 m | 3.90 t | 64.1 % | PASS || 2 | 3.0 t | 25 m | 3.00 t | 100.0 % | PASS || 3 | 4.0 t | 30 m | 2.50 t | 160.0 % | FAIL |## Heaviest erection component- **MCT 88:** counter-jib, 4.38 t (variant A, 11.9 m), datasheet p. 6; variant B is 4.25 t at the same  11.9 m. The build does **not** copy`docs/`, so the source PDFs stay out of the distributable. The build fails if a PDF appears anywherein the copied plugin directory.## Limitations- Four catalogue entries are present: two sourced Potain trolley-jib cranes and two `INDICATIVE`  JASO luffing-jib configurations. The JASO rows are listed but **not evaluated** by the  trolley-jib capacity engine; their capacity charts are stored, but `capacityAt` refuses them and  picks are `UNSUPPORTED`. See [`docs/SOURCES.md`](docs/SOURCES.md) §6.- The MCT 88 and MCT 85 F5 30 m default jib is `INDICATIVE` project configuration, not a manufacturer  recommendation. The JASO 213 ft TD/2R configuration choice is `INDICATIVE` for the same reason: the  datasheet publishes other jib lengths.- The model is not a structural, dynamic, reeving or ground-bearing check.- The MCT 88 source documents disagree about the 30 m tip value (2.70 t label vs 2.54 t imperial  conversion, and a 2.5 t point on the metric curve page). The load-curve page is also recorded as  p. 3 in the Cycle 2 copy and p. 4 in the local reference copy; that page-number ambiguity is  unresolved and is not guessed. The chosen value and the discrepancies are documented in  [`docs/SOURCES.md`](docs/SOURCES.md) §3.- The MCT 85 F5 p. 1 tip table labels 25 m as 3.0 t, while the p. 3 chart prints 3.02 t. The tip table  keeps the p. 1 label; the capacity lookup uses the p. 3 point.- `BACKLOG.md` refers to `docs/validation-calculation.txt`. No original was found in the working tree  or Git history; [`docs/validation-calculation.txt`](docs/validation-calculation.txt) is a clearly  labelled **reconstruction**, not the recovered original. See [`docs/SOURCES.md`](docs/SOURCES.md) §7.- The JASO mast width, base width, counter-jib structural length, counter-jib radius and heaviest  erection component are `notStated` because the inspected PDF does not publish them. They are not  inferred from other dimensions or from the combined transport package.- The MCT 85 F5 and Jaso PDFs do not state a redistribution licence. Confirm the manufacturer's terms  before packaging or mirroring them. The PDFs are ignored by Git, and the production build copies  only `manifest.json`, `icons/` and `dist/`, so they are not in `dist/plugins/tower-planner/`.
